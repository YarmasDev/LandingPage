Data We Collect

To provide our consolidated analysis services of your Amazon performance, we collect and process the following types of data:

Amazon Ads Account

Advertising Performance Metrics: PPC spend, PPC sales, ads clicks, impressions, and PPC purchases.

Source: This information is obtained directly from the Amazon Ads API.

Data from Your Amazon Seller/Vendor Account (SP-API)

Product Sales and Traffic Metrics: total sales, units, sessions, click count, impression count, add to cart count, purchase count, search traffics sales, ordered product sales, buy box percentage, browser session, browser views, mobile sessions, and mobile views.

Source: This information is obtained directly from the Selling Partner API (SP-API).

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively for the following purposes:

Service Provision and Improvement: To operate, maintain, protect, and improve our platform and the services we offer.

Consolidated Client Analysis: To combine data from your AmazonAds account and your Amazon Seller/Vendor account, allowing us to provide you with comprehensive analysis and a unified view of your product and campaign performance.

Custom Reports and Dashboards: To generate reports, dashboards, and visualizations that help you better understand your business metrics.

Optimization and Recommendations: To identify trends, opportunities, and offer personalized recommendations that can help you optimize your sales and advertising strategies.

Communication: To send you important notifications about the service, updates, alerts, and to respond to your inquiries.

Internal Purposes: For internal audits, troubleshooting, and the development of new features.
